About this site

The Oregon Election Atlas is an independent tracker of Oregon elections. It is not affiliated with the Oregon Secretary of State, any county elections office, or any news organization.

Election night results are unofficial

Vote counts come from the Oregon Secretary of State’s unofficial results site, results.oregonvotes.gov, read about every 15 minutes from 8:00 PM on election night. Counties keep counting for days after the election, and results become official only when the Secretary of State certifies them. Always check the official site before relying on a number.

How races are called here

Race calls on this site are its own projections, not official results and not calls by any news organization. A race is called when the leader cannot be caught: either the lead is larger than the estimated number of ballots still to be counted, or the runner-up would need at least 75% of the remaining ballots to catch up, and no projection is made before about 30% of the expected vote is counted. Races with a single candidate on the ballot are called when polls close. Calls are reviewed by a person before they are published and can be withdrawn if the count changes.

The estimate of ballots still to be counted uses each district’s registered voters and statewide ballot returns, padded so that it errs on the side of waiting. It is an estimate; the share counted shown for each race is approximate.

The desk feed

Posts in the Atlas Desk feed are written by this site to summarize the results it shows. They are not from an official account or any real social media account.

District data

  • Voter registration: Oregon Secretary of State monthly registration reports.
  • Past results: county canvass results compiled by OpenElections, checked against the Secretary of State’s certified abstracts.
  • Statewide races by district: precinct results summed to each district; precincts split between districts are divided in proportion to their own legislative vote.
  • District boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau 2022 cartographic boundary files (the maps adopted in 2021).
  • Candidates on the 2026 ballot: compiled from public candidate lists; confirm against the official Voters’ Pamphlet.
  • Legislators: Open States; members of Congress: the congress-legislators project.
